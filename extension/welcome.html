✦ Lumen

Record a tab or your screen with your voice, then save it as an MP4. Open source (MIT).

What Lumen captures, and where it goes

  • Only while you record: the tab, window or screen you choose, its sound when Chrome allows it, your mic if it’s on, and your camera if the face bubble is on.
  • The face bubble finds you in the camera picture with an AI model that runs inside Lumen, on this computer.
  • Recordings are saved in Chrome’s storage for Lumen on this computer, and stay there until you download or delete them, shared or not.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on share links, and then only to your own Cloudflare account. There is no Lumen server. No accounts, no analytics.

Let Lumen use your mic and camera

Chrome asks once. Lumen records in the background, where Chrome can’t ask, so it asks here. The camera is only for the face bubble, which is off until you turn it on.

How to record

  • Click ✦ in the toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu), choose This tab or Screen or window, and press Start.
  • To stop: click ✦ again, press Alt+Shift+S (change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts), or use Chrome’s “Stop sharing”.
  • Your recording opens in a new tab, ready to download.

Share links (optional)

Get a link like Loom’s the moment you stop: it’s copied right away and the video uploads in the background. Links live on your own share site, a free Cloudflare worker in your account. Already using Lumen on Omarchy? Connect the share site you have.
