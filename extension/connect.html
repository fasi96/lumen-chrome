✦ Share links

Lumen puts the videos you share on your own Cloudflare account (free for most people). There is no Lumen server: the link, the video and the stats are yours. Without a share site, recordings still save as MP4s on this computer.

Disconnecting removes this computer’s key from your share site. Videos you already shared stay.

I already have a Lumen share site

On the computer where you set it up (Omarchy), run this in a terminal, then paste the code here:

lumen share connect-code --copy

It makes a key just for this browser (you can revoke it on your dashboard under Devices). Your share site’s main key stays on that computer.

I don’t have one yet

Lumen can set up your share site on your Cloudflare account for you, in a few minutes, without a terminal. Cloudflare asks for a payment method on file to switch on video storage (R2); it stays free up to 10 GB, and watching videos is always free.
