✦ Set up your share site

Lumen makes your share site on your own Cloudflare account: a small program (a “worker”) that serves your links, plus storage for the videos (R2) and a little database (D1). There is no Lumen server, and nothing goes anywhere but your account. It’s free for most people.

1. Let Lumen set it up, once

  1. Cloudflare needs an account (free): sign up if you don’t have one.
  2. Switch on R2 (video storage). Cloudflare asks for a payment method on file to do that, even though it stays free up to 10 GB of videos and watching them is always free. Open R2 in Cloudflare.
  3. Make an API token for Lumen. This button opens Cloudflare’s page with the right permissions already filled in:
    Optionally set “TTL” to end today: Lumen only needs it for the next few minutes and doesn’t keep it.
